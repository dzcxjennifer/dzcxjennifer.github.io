# About
I am a third year PhD candidate in Operations Research at Stanford University, fortunate to be advised by Itai Ashlagi. I work on market design and optimization. 

I received my bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (with Departmental Citation) from UC Berkeley, where I worked with Anil Aswani, Abhishek Nagaraj, and Cheng Ren at Berkeley.
